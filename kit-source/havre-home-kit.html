The HAVRE Home Kit

To help your child show what they feel, even when the words won’t come, and calm down when things boil over. No screens, starting tonight.

Four golden rules

Introduce the kit at a calm moment.Never in the middle of a meltdown: you learn to swim away from the storm.
The gauge belongs to your child.Don’t correct their colour. You can simply say: “Thanks for showing me.”
No grades, no rewards tied to the colour.Red isn’t “bad”. It’s information.
Look for the “before”, not someone to blame.What happened just before is the most valuable clue.

Your trial week

Day 1Print, put the gauge up and fill in the plan (page 3) together.
Days 2 to 6Your child moves their arrow whenever they want. The mission cards are for when “there’s nothing to do”.
Every evening30 seconds in the notebook (page 5), only if the gauge went up.
Day 7The check-in (page 6), together, and your feedback to help us.

This kit is an everyday support tool. It is not a medical tool and does not replace professional advice. If you are worried about your child, talk to their doctor or a health professional.

For the parent · 30 seconds in the evening

The notebook: what happened just before?

Only note the moments when the gauge went up (orange or red). No need to write everything: a few words are enough.

When? Just before… (where, with whom, what activity, tired or hungry?) Colour What helped them calm down
E.g. Tuesday, 4:45 pm Back from school, math homework, hadn’t eaten.
A snack, then 10 min outside. Homework after.
Look for patterns, not culprits. A transition, a time of day, a person, hunger… What keeps coming back is a real clue.
Useful at appointments. If you see a professional, this notebook helps them understand in 2 minutes what happens at home. You decide whether to show it.
