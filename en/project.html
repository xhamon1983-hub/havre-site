The HAVRE project

The kit is the first step.

Eventually, HAVRE will be an app for the child, an app for the parent and a tool for the professionals who support them. We’ll build it from what families teach us with the kit.

For the child

An app that speaks their language.

The kit’s gauge, made digital: a swipe of the thumb, a colour, and a buddy by their side.

  • A gauge to slide with a finger, from green to red, to say how they’re doing without searching for words.
  • Short missions to give structure to empty moments: morning, after school, evening.
  • A mascot they choose, showing small words of support at the right moment.

For the parent

Understand, without monitoring.

The kit’s notebook, made better: landmarks through the day and a way to be there, even from a distance.

  • See when it builds up and when it settles, and spot what keeps coming back: homework, transitions, tiredness.
  • Send a gentle message, shown by the mascot: “Thinking of you.” “We’ll talk about it calmly later.”
  • Stay consistent between parents: same landmarks, same tone.
For professionals

HAVRE Pro: a shared language with the family.

For psychologists, special education technicians, educators and schools. Insight into the child’s daily life, never a diagnosis.

  • A contextualised emotional curve over a day, week or month, to prepare a meeting with concrete examples.
  • Access granted by the parent, with a time-limited key that can be revoked at any time.
  • Everyone sees what they need: an educator doesn’t see the same things as a psychologist.

Our commitments

Help without monitoring, support without replacing.

Not a medical toolHAVRE doesn’t diagnose and doesn’t replace a doctor or a therapist.
The gauge belongs to the childOnly they move it. There’s no right or wrong colour.
No scores, no rankingsMissions are landmarks, not a system of rewards or punishments.
Not a spyNo location tracking, no control over the child’s apps.
Minimal dataEncrypted, never sold, and deletable at any time.
The parent stays in controlProfessionals’ access is temporary and revocable in one click.
