For the child

An app that speaks their language.

The kit’s gauge becomes a swipe of the thumb. A buddy chosen by the child stays by their side and helps them calm down when things build up.

For the parent

Understand, without monitoring.

The kit’s notebook, made better: see when things build up, spot what keeps coming back, and be there even from a distance.

For professionals

HAVRE Pro: a shared language with the family.

For psychologists, special education technicians, educators and schools. The parent shares time-limited access: the professional comes to the meeting with concrete examples, never with a diagnosis.

Our approach

How we’re building HAVRE.

📄 On paper first

Before writing code, we check with the kit that the ideas really help: the gauge, the calm-down plan, the parent’s notebook.

👨‍👩‍👧 With families and professionals

Every piece of feedback counts. The families testing the kit, then the pilot families, decide what goes into the app.

🔒 Safety from day one

Safeguards aren’t added at the end: they shape every screen. See our commitments

Our commitments

Help without monitoring, support without replacing.

Not a medical toolHAVRE doesn’t diagnose and doesn’t replace a doctor or a therapist.
The gauge belongs to the childOnly they move it. There’s no right or wrong colour.
No scores, no rankingsMissions are landmarks, not a system of rewards or punishments.
Not a spyNo location tracking, no control over the child’s apps.
Minimal dataEncrypted, never sold, and deletable at any time.
The parent stays in controlProfessionals’ access is temporary and revocable in one click.

FAQ

About the HAVRE app

When will the app be available?

We don’t have a date yet. The prototype will come after feedback from the families testing the kit, then it will be tried by pilot families before opening to everyone.

Which devices?

The app is designed first for iPhone and Apple Watch. A dedicated watch or wristband version will come later.

Are the screens on this page final?

No. They’re our mockups. They will evolve with what families and professionals teach us.

Who is behind HAVRE?

A parent who lived through their child’s blow-ups, building HAVRE with other families and support professionals.

Will my child’s data be protected?

Yes: minimal data, encrypted, never sold, and deletable at any time. Professionals only get access if you give them a time-limited key. See our commitments.
