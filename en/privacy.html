Privacy

What we do with your information

Last updated: November 9, 2026

HAVRE is a project run by a parent, in Quebec, Canada. We collect the bare minimum, and this page explains it in plain words. For any question, write to contact.havre@icloud.com: this is also the address of the person responsible for the protection of personal information at HAVRE.

What we collect

  • When you request the kit: your email address (required) and, if you wish, your first name, your child’s age range and how you heard about HAVRE.
  • When you fill in the “Get involved” form: your email address, your role (parent, professional, other) and, if you wish, your first name, your city and a message.
  • When you write to us: the content of your message and whatever you choose to put in it.

We never ask for your child’s name, nor any health information.

Why

  • To send you the kit and, a week later, ask how it went.
  • To give you news about the project, at most once a month.
  • To answer your request if you want to take part in the project.
  • To understand, overall and anonymously, how old the children are and where families come from, so we can improve the kit.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell, rent or share your information with anyone.
  • We don’t send advertising.
  • We don’t use any tracking cookies or advertising pixels.

Where your information is kept

  • The site’s forms are handled by Netlify, the site’s host, whose servers are located in the United States. Netlify stores the responses in a space reserved for HAVRE.
  • Our email exchanges go through an iCloud (Apple) mailbox.
  • Visit statistics are measured by GoatCounter, a tool that sets no cookies and cannot identify you: it counts visits, page views and country, without storing IP addresses.

For how long

We keep your information as long as the HAVRE project is active and you have not asked for it to be deleted. If the project stops, it will be deleted.

Your rights

At any time you can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, withdraw your consent or have it deleted. Simply write to contact.havre@icloud.com from the address concerned. We reply within 30 days at most.

The kit itself

The kit is a paper document. What you and your child write in it stays at home: nothing is sent to HAVRE, except what you choose to tell us by email.

Changes

If this page changes significantly, we will say so in our next message to registered families. The date at the top of the page shows the last update.
