Inside the kit

Simple tools your child can actually touch.

No theory to read: pages to put up, fill in together and cut out.

The fridge gauge

An arrow to move. One colour, one gesture, no words needed.

My calm-down plan

Made while calm, with a secret word that means “I need help”.

12 mission cards

For empty moments, when pressure builds for lack of structure.

The parent’s notebook

30 seconds in the evening to spot what happens just before.

+ a one-page guide to get started, and a check-in sheet for the end of the week.

How it works

One week to try it.

Print and put it up

Read the guide (2 minutes), then put the gauge on the fridge.

Make the plan while calm

On a good day, fill in “My calm-down plan” together. Never in the middle of a meltdown.

Live the week, then check in

Your child moves their arrow whenever they want. On day 7, you look together at what helped.

Why a paper kit

And not (yet) an app?

  • Because it works tonight.Nothing to install, not one more screen. A sheet on the fridge is visible to the whole family.
  • Because your child stays in charge.The gauge is theirs. No grades, no rewards: just a simple way to be understood.
  • Because your feedback shapes what comes next.The HAVRE app will be built on what actually helps families, not on our guesses.

FAQ

Before you start

Is it really free?

Yes. In return, we simply ask you to tell us, after a week, what helped and what was missing.

What age is it for?

The kit is designed for children aged about 6 to 12. With younger kids, read the cards together. Older ones can adapt the missions their own way.

Does it replace professional support?

No. The kit is an everyday support tool, not a medical tool. If you’re worried about your child, talk to their doctor or a professional. The parent’s notebook can even help them understand in two minutes what happens at home.

What do you do with my email?

We only use it to write to you about the kit and the HAVRE project, once a month at most. It is never sold or shared, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

I’m a professional (therapist, educator, teacher…): can I share it?

Please do! Just share the link to this page with the families you support. To talk with us, write to contact.havre@icloud.com.

What about the HAVRE app?

It’s in the works, and the kit is the first step. Discover the project.
